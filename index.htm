Session Cookies
Copy your ALLDATA session cookies from the browser inspector and add them here. They are stored encrypted and injected automatically into every employee's app.
📋 How to Copy Your ALLDATA Cookies
1
Open your browser → go to my.alldata.com → log in normally
2
Press F12 to open DevTools → click Application tab (Chrome) or Storage tab (Firefox)
3
On the left sidebar → click Cookies → click https://my.alldata.com
4
You will see a list of cookies. Add the important ones below — especially any with names like session, auth, token, or ASPXAUTH
Name │ Value
──────────────────┼──────────────────
.ASPXAUTH │ ABC123xyz...
ASP.NET_SessionId │ qwerty123...
authToken │ eyJhbG...
5
Copy each cookie name and value → add them below → click Save All Cookies
